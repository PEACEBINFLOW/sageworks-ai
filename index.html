Mission

Exploring what happens when computation becomes temporal — sequences of events that can be measured, replayed, and reasoned about.

🕐 Time-Native by Default

LAW-T, LAW-N, and MindsEye assume that when something happened is as important as what happened. Time is a first-class type.

🌐 Networks as Laws

LAW-N treats routing as a legal system: who can send what to whom, under which temporal and contextual constraints.

🧠 Agents That Remember

MindsEye gives agents a ledger-style memory: perception becomes a queryable stream, not ephemeral JSON.

💫 Data as Economy

An ecosystem where data moves, trades, scores and evolves — all plugged into one temporal fabric.

Research

Frameworks and theory that aren't shipped as products yet — the substrate everything else is built on top of.

DBI — Dimensional Binary Interpreter

Research

Transforms binary strings into coordinate representations through a dimensional separator notation, and is being pushed toward a physical substrate — a chip whose gate pattern performs the transform directly, or a central dispatch chip that routes tasks by DBI coordinate math. Gaming and MMORPG interaction data is the likely proving ground for the whole architecture.

The gaming stack was never a side project for DBI — it's the proving ground.

Spacetime Binary Reframe

Research

Reframes binary's 1 and 0 as space (position) and the time it takes to traverse into that position, instead of the standard presence/absence reading. It's foundational to how Ledgrund is designed at the ground level.

One and zero were never about presence and absence. They're about space and the time it takes to close.

Ledgrund — Binary-Native Math

Research

A math system where binary is the native ground-level language rather than an implementation detail. Three resident AIs — Aperture, Warden, and Keeper — handle projection, translation, and ledger state, and the first working build shipped as an offline, single-ZIP Windows 10 app covering the four basic operations.

Getting an answer wrong the right way teaches Ledgrund more than getting it right.

Library Ground / Aerie

Research

A ledger-native offline cognitive engine that runs as a command-prompt system, not a website. Four named AI "eagles" — Herald, Meridian, Cairn, and Forge — each keep their own ledger and talk to each other over a separate machine-language protocol, shipped as a Windows 10-native PowerShell build.

Four eagles, four ledgers, and a machine language the humans never have to read.

Player-Authority MMORPG Design

Research

A design for graduated, GM-like player authority instead of developer-run live content — authority scales an NPC's power ceiling, unlocks a dedicated AI agent, and eventually converts into a territory status window that levels up with the player.

Authority isn't unlocked by the developer. It's earned, and it scales the world back.

SAGEWORKS Data Research Labs

Research

A proposed research arm reading Reddit, Discord, X, GitHub, and Product Hunt for traffic and ranking signals, feeding topics to SAGEWORKS AI's generation engine. The core argument: Botswana talent is real but structurally under-visible online, and the fix is organizing local data rather than competing on data centers.

Botswana's talent isn't missing. The traffic data just never learned where to look.

LAW-N / MindsEye / N-SQL / LAW-T

Research

The original SAGEWORKS research substrate. LAW-N treats every interaction as a time-labeled signal crossing a graph of agents and devices; MindsEye turns inboxes and logs into one continuous perception stream; N-SQL and LAW-T extend the same time-native model into query and programming languages.

Every inbox, log, and ledger is one perception stream if you stop treating it as scattered tables.

VOICEKIN

Practical

The flagship consumer-facing project: a consented AI voice/persona system for short-form video. Users upload their own consented clips, the system separates voice and language properties, and new lines get synthesized onto that voice across three layers — app intelligence, a per-user local ledger, and a consented voice marketplace.

Starting fully offline, seeded by one consenting voice, before this touches a network at all.

Three fridges, one ledger, and a pricing history that tells its own story.
